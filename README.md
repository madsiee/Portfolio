# My Portfolio
Mahdieh Dehghan 
This is my personal portfolio site, built as an immersive, scroll-driven journey rather than a static page. As you scroll, you fly through a starfield past sections covering who I am, what I do, and the work I've shipped — with each section arriving as its own floating panel in 3D space, the ambient background shifting color as you move deeper in, and a starfield that reacts subtly to your cursor throughout.

I'm a Marketing & Brand Manager with 6+ years of experience driving growth, brand strategy, and digital transformation for FinTech, Web3, and eCommerce companies. My work spans marketing operations, customer acquisition, UX research, and content strategy — with a consistent throughline of translating ambiguous problems into clear, data-backed direction. This portfolio walks through that experience: a timeline of roles, key metrics from past campaigns, and a set of case studies covering brand identity, e-commerce, and exhibition design work.

Built with React, TypeScript, Vite, and Three.js (via React Three Fiber), the site treats scrolling as a physical, continuous motion rather than a series of jump cuts — every panel, star, and color transition is driven by a single scroll-progress value, so the whole experience stays fluid and connected from the intro to the contact section at the end.
Immersive space portfolio inspired by [dungyov.com](https://dungyov.com).
